# Bahubhashi India

*Visualizing India’s Spoken Language Landscape*

How many languages does each Indian state speak, and what do they sound like?
An interactive map of mother tongues from the 2011 Census, with short speech samples of each state's main language.

A spinout from [SPIN Lab](https://spinlabiitg.github.io/), Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial
Intelligence, IIT Guwahati, India.

This folder is generated by `scripts/build_public.py`; edit the source in `site/` and rebuild.

## Data and credits
- **Languages:** Census of India 2011, *Language: India, States and Union Territories* (Paper 1 of 2018),
  Table C-16 and Statement 9. Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India.
- **Speech:** [Vaani](https://vaani.iisc.ac.in/), IISc Bangalore and ARTPARK,
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Clips trimmed to 5-10 s and loudness-matched;
  only reviewed clips are included.
- **Boundaries:** [INDIAN-SHAPEFILES](https://github.com/datta07/INDIAN-SHAPEFILES), MIT licence
  (see `licenses/`), merged to 2011 census units. Boundaries are approximate and for illustration only.
- **Scripts:** [Unicode CLDR](https://cldr.unicode.org/) likely scripts; letters in Noto fonts (SIL OFL) via Google Fonts.
- **Software:** MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause), H3 (Apache 2.0).

## Disclaimer
A research and educational visualization, not an official publication of the Government of India or the Census.
Speaker numbers are reproduced from the 2011 Census as published; language names, groupings and families are the census's.
Boundaries are approximate and for illustration only, and are not an authoritative depiction of India's borders.
Voice clips are short samples of individual speakers. Provided as is, without warranty; corrections are welcome.
